Hi! I am currently a Postdoc/Research Assistant Professor at TTIC.

Previously, I was a PhD student in the Theory Group at CMU, advised by Pravesh Kothari. Throughout my grad school, I have spent most of my time thinking about convex relaxation hierarchies and their limitations, as well as the intersection of TCS and random matrix theory.

Prior to CMU, I obtained my B.A. in Mathematics at UC Berkeley, where I had the fortune of working with Prasad Raghavendra. I am also grateful for the mentorship of Siu On Chan at CUHK.
